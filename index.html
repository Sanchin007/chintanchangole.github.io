DELIVERY MODEL

A repeatable operating model from frontline observation to measurable adoption.

The sequence matters. I avoid automating a broken process by diagnosing and measuring first, then designing controls, validating the solution and using adoption/support data as the next improvement signal.

01
DISCOVER

Shadow & map

Observe real workflows, handoffs, exceptions and repetitive work with functional leads and super users.

02
QUANTIFY

Measure friction

Use SQL, Python, deep-dive reports and dashboards to establish baseline volume, time, quality and operational impact.

03
SPECIFY

Translate to requirements

Convert bottlenecks into functional specifications, rules, controls and acceptance criteria that engineering teams can build.

04
ENGINEER

Build / automate

Configure back-office applications and workflow automation using Python, SQL, APIs, low-code and AI-assisted steps where appropriate.

05
VALIDATE

Test & release

Coordinate feature testing, UAT, exception handling, controls and production readiness before operational handoff.

06
ADOPT

Train & improve

Deliver documentation, training and support; track usage, quality and tickets; feed recurring friction back into the backlog.
